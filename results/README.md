# Results

Public-safe deliverables from interactive and scheduled tasks belong here. Use a dated, descriptive filename and include:

- the task and scope;
- the outcome;
- sources or public artifact URLs;
- external actions actually taken;
- uncertainties or follow-up work; and
- the completion date.

Raw pages, screenshots, private forum content, cookies, command logs, and transcripts belong under ignored `.pi-data/`, not here. Reusable lessons may additionally qualify for curated `memory/`.
